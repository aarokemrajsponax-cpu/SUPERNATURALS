PROJECT DIRECTIVE: THE ULTIMATE AAA-INSPIRED ROBLOX GAME TRANSFORMATION
FULL-SCALE QUALITY OVERHAUL • COMBAT REENGINEERING • VISUAL REVOLUTION • RELEASE READINESS
Claude, this is a critical development directive. Read the entire document before making changes. Every section matters.
Claude,
We missed our Halloween release target. We now have a very aggressive release deadline of November 3, 2026. We cannot afford to spend our remaining development resources carelessly, and we absolutely cannot afford another update that merely makes the game look slightly different without fundamentally improving its quality.
I need you to approach this project with the mindset of a world-class AAA game development studio.
This is not a request for a few minor adjustments, random visual effects, or superficial UI improvements.
THIS IS A COMPREHENSIVE GAME QUALITY TRANSFORMATION.
I need you to take the existing game and elevate it into something extraordinarily polished, immersive, visually impressive, addictive in the healthy sense of being genuinely enjoyable to revisit, responsive, intuitive, and memorable.
Our aspirational quality benchmark is the level of production polish associated with games such as GTA VI: exceptional environmental presentation, carefully constructed visuals, responsive animation, coherent art direction, high-quality interfaces, immersive worlds, and an experience that feels intentionally designed.
Obviously, translate that ambition into what is technically achievable within Roblox. Do not simply claim that something is AAA quality. Demonstrate it through the actual implementation.
I want this game to stand out from ordinary Roblox experiences.
I want players to enter the experience and immediately recognize that considerably more thought, artistry, technical care, and development effort have gone into it than they are accustomed to seeing.
Your objective is to make the existing game feel like a premium, professionally developed superpower combat experience.
Do not waste development resources generating unnecessary videos. We do not need video generation for this update.
Work efficiently. Inspect existing systems before modifying them, reuse high-quality existing assets where appropriate, avoid unnecessary rewrites, and keep development operations focused. Use specialized or parallel agents only when they can handle genuinely independent tasks without creating integration problems.
Most importantly, do not sacrifice functionality for appearances or appearances for functionality. We need BOTH.

1. DEVELOPMENT PHILOSOPHY AND NON-NEGOTIABLE QUALITY STANDARDS
Before you begin, inspect the existing project thoroughly.
Understand:
The existing game architecture and folder structure.
Current combat and ability systems.
Fighter and degree selection.
Lobby and training mechanics.
Existing monetization and player-data systems.
Map construction, environmental assets, and player bases.
UI architecture and existing menus.
Current animations, particles, sounds, and visual effects.
Existing scripts, dependencies, and known errors.
Do not blindly replace functional systems.
Preserve working functionality while improving weak implementations. Where existing architecture is fundamentally responsible for poor quality, refactor it properly rather than layering unreliable fixes over it.
Quality standards
Every new or redesigned feature must satisfy these principles:
Intentional design: Everything must have a purpose, visual identity, and coherent relationship to the overall game.
Professional execution: No cheap-looking placeholders, unfinished geometry, default-looking interfaces, awkward animations, or disconnected visual effects.
Consistency: UI, environments, abilities, lighting, colors, animation, typography, and interactions must feel like they belong to the same game.
Responsiveness: Interactions must feel immediate, natural, and satisfying.
Technical stability: No broken systems, avoidable performance problems, exploitable purchases, or unreliable mechanics.
Accessibility: Players must understand the experience without needing to guess how anything works.
Replayability: The experience should provide genuine reasons to return, improve, explore, train, customize, and compete.
Do not interpret "make it better" as permission to add random neon lights, enormous particle emitters, excessive bloom, or unnecessary animations.
We need exceptional artistic direction, not visual clutter.
The goal is a recognizable, distinctive visual identity that looks professionally art-directed.

Claude, I am especially dissatisfied with the current attack presentation.
The attack effects look underdeveloped, and combat does not currently deliver the excitement or visual impact that this game needs.
This must change substantially.
I want combat to feel like participating in a beautifully choreographed, cinematic superpower battle.
Use the general appeal of highly responsive Roblox arena fighters as inspiration, but develop our own distinctive combat identity and visual language.
We need exceptional responsiveness, movement, animations, effects, and satisfying feedback.
The combat system must be one of the strongest parts of this entire experience.

9. ATTACK TARGETING AND PLAYER FREEDOM
I noticed that triggering many attacks currently requires facing the opponent.
I want a more advanced targeting system, particularly for Corruption, Syntax, and Admin.
Corruption, Syntax, and Admin
Implement click-to-target ability casting.
The intended interaction:
The player activates an ability.
The player enters an appropriate targeting mode.
A clear aiming indicator appears.
The player chooses the location where the ability should be released.
The ability activates at the selected location.
The player receives appropriate visual and audio feedback.
Make the targeting system smooth and intuitive.
Players should feel like they have control over where their powers go rather than being forced into a rigid facing-direction system.
Provide cancel functionality, appropriate range limits, and clear feedback.
Support keyboard, mouse, mobile touch, and gamepad where possible.
Other fighters
Different powers should have different control experiences.
Strong Dude and Grand Master:
Primarily use directional attacks and opponent-facing mechanics. Keep these intuitive and responsive.
Machine Master:
Introduce appropriate projectile aiming and click-to-direct firing interactions.
Ninja:
Introduce more interactive movement, controlled teleport targeting, and directional slashing interactions.
Do not force every fighter into the same targeting model.
Each fighting style should feel distinct.

10. COMBAT ENERGY, COOLDOWNS, AND GAMEPLAY FREEDOM
The current system restricts attacks too much through energy consumption.

I want:
Much lower energy consumption.
Faster regeneration.
Less unnecessary waiting.
More opportunities to chain abilities.
More accessible basic attacks.
Greater flexibility during combat.
Do not create an experience in which players spend most of their time waiting for abilities to become available.
At the same time, preserve meaningful combat balance.
Use carefully tuned cooldowns, recovery windows, and ability constraints rather than making every powerful attack completely unlimited.
The goal is fluid, exciting combat without frustrating restrictions or uncontrollable ability spam.
Create configurable combat values so we can adjust fighter balance without rewriting entire scripts.

13. FIGHTER AND DEGREE SELECTION: REBUILD THE SYSTEM
The existing fighter-selection and degree-selection experience needs a much better structure.
I want a clear distinction between:
THE LOBBY, TRAINING, AND ACTUAL COMBAT.
Lobby rules
Players must not have active combat abilities simply because they are standing in the lobby.
The lobby is a preparation and social space.
Players can:
Explore the facility.
Visit stores.
Check their progress.
Preview fighters.
Access training.
Prepare for a match.
Customize permitted settings.
However, they cannot use their purchased combat powers in the lobby.
Fighter and degree selection should happen when players are ready to enter combat, through the designated pre-match selection flow.
Do not allow players to bypass this restriction through client-side manipulation.
Training rules
Training is where players can experiment.
Training degrees and training powers must be free.
Players should be able to experiment with different abilities without spending their in-game currency or Robux.
Training should provide an appropriate place to learn controls, test abilities, and understand how fighters operate.
Training access must be clearly separated from competitive match selection.

14. DEGREE PRICING AND MONETIZATION
We need a clearly structured progression and monetization system.
The economy should be understandable, reliable, and properly integrated.
Standard degrees
Higher-tier degrees and powers must require progressively higher in-game currency payments when selected or switched to.
The price should correspond to the degree's tier and progression value.
Every selection screen must show:
Degree name.
Description.
Ability preview.
Current ownership or unlock status.
Exact selection price.
Whether the selection is permanent or temporary.
Whether the player can afford it.
The player must see the cost before confirming.
Apply the appropriate charge when a player confirms a qualifying selection or switch.
Do not accidentally charge players simply for browsing, previewing, or opening menus.
Corruption, Syntax, and Admin
These three special degrees must use a separate unlocking structure.
Each requires a one-time Robux unlock payment.
Once the player has unlocked the appropriate special degree, it should remain owned according to the game's purchase entitlement system.
However, selecting or switching to these special degrees must also use their designated in-game currency prices, with the costs clearly displayed.
Keep the Robux unlock and the in-game selection cost separate.
Additional ability slots
Consider an optional Robux purchase that unlocks one or two additional ability slots.
However:
Show the exact purchase contents.
Make the purchase completely optional.
Preserve a viable free progression experience.
Do not create an unavoidable pay-to-win advantage.
Clearly communicate what is being purchased.
Technical requirements
All purchases and currency deductions must be secure.
Use server-authoritative purchase verification and properly implemented Roblox-supported commerce systems.
Persist entitlements and player progression reliably.
Prevent duplicate charges, invalid deductions, purchase spoofing, and accidental loss of ownership.
The selection and purchase interface must be visually excellent and completely understandable.
