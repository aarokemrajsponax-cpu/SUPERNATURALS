ROBLOX GAME DEVELOPMENT PROMPT: MASTER KUNG FU / MARTIAL ARTIST

1. CORE CONCEPT — THE GRANDMASTER

Create a highly polished, skill-based martial arts supernatural power for my Roblox fighting game. This character is a legendary hand-to-hand combat master who has perfected striking, counterattacking, redirection, evasive movement, pressure-point techniques, and devastating martial arts finishers.

The character should feel like a true martial arts grandmaster: fast, composed, precise, and terrifyingly effective in close combat.

The defining fantasy: The player doesn’t need swords, guns, or giant energy blasts. Their body is their weapon. Every punch, kick, parry, dodge, and counter should feel deliberate, powerful, and satisfying.

The gameplay should combine:

* Fluid kung fu combos.
* High-impact punches, kicks, elbows, knees, and sweeps.
* Perfect-timing parries and counterattacks.
* Advanced movement, dashes, and evasive techniques.
* Cinematic ultimate abilities.
* Progression that rewards mechanical skill rather than button mashing.

Build this as a complete, playable supernatural power within the existing game’s power-degree progression system. Do not create multiple separate characters for the individual fighting styles.

2. COMBAT DESIGN

Every attack must have a distinct animation, sound, hit effect, hit reaction, and gameplay purpose.

Basic Combat — The Iron Fist

Create a responsive basic attack system with a four- to five-hit combo chain.

1. Opening Strike: A fast straight punch that starts the combo.
2. Twin Dragon Fists: Two rapid alternating punches that maintain pressure.
3. Rising Palm: An upward palm strike that briefly launches the opponent.
4. Whirlwind Kick: A rotating kick that knocks the opponent backward and ends the chain.

If the player lands the final strike, award a satisfying hit-stop effect, a short camera shake, impact particles, and a powerful sound effect.

Add combo timing windows, recovery frames, hit-stun limits, and defensive counterplay. Repeated attacks should not create infinite stun-locks.

Advanced Martial Arts Techniques

Develop the following techniques as distinct abilities.

A. Dragon’s Step — Mobility

The player performs a rapid directional dash that can weave around an opponent’s attack.

* Short, responsive movement burst.
* Subtle afterimage trail.
* No long-distance teleportation.
* Brief recovery period.
* A successful dodge near an incoming attack grants a small counterattack opportunity.

B. Tiger Claw — Guard Breaker

A forceful palm-and-claw combination designed to pressure defensive opponents.

* Two quick body strikes followed by a powerful palm thrust.
* Increased guard pressure.
* Moderate knockback on the final hit.
* Clearly telegraphed final strike.
* Blockable or dodgeable with correct timing.

C. Crane’s Reversal — Perfect Counter

The player enters a short defensive stance. If an enemy strikes during the active counter window, the player redirects the attack and retaliates with a precise martial arts combination.

* Reward timing and awareness.
* Successful counters produce a distinctive sound and brief visual flash.
* Failed counters leave the player vulnerable for a short period.
* Cannot counter grabs or explicitly unblockable attacks unless designed for that interaction.

D. Iron Mountain — Heavy Strike

A devastating close-range shoulder check followed by a heavy palm strike.

* Short wind-up with a readable animation.
* High impact and guard pressure.
* Strong knockback.
* Limited turning during the committed attack.
* Punishable if the player misses.

E. Sweeping Dragon — Crowd Control

A low spinning sweep that can catch nearby opponents.

* Moderate area of effect.
* Brief knockdown.
* Useful against groups without instantly defeating everyone.
* Opponents can jump, evade, or defend according to the move’s intended counterplay.

F. Hundred Fists — Combo Technique

The player unleashes a rapid, choreographed series of punches, elbows, and palm strikes against a nearby target.

* Requires a successful opening hit or appropriate range.
* Multiple individually timed impacts.
* Final strike launches the target away.
* The target is not locked into an excessively long animation.
* Other players can interrupt the move before its confirmed opening or exploit its recovery afterward.

3. POWER DEGREES — MARTIAL ARTS MASTERY

Power degrees are progression stages of the SAME character. Each degree unlocks new techniques, upgrades existing techniques, and changes the character’s visual presentation.

Do not treat the degrees as separate characters.

Degree I — Disciple

The player has learned the fundamentals.

Unlock:

* Basic martial arts combo.
* Dragon’s Step.
* Tiger Claw.

Visual identity:

* Clean, restrained animations.
* Small impact particles.
* Traditional martial arts stance.
* No excessive supernatural effects.

Gameplay identity: Fast, approachable close-range combat.

Degree II — Adept

The player has mastered defensive timing and advanced combinations.

Unlock:

* Crane’s Reversal.
* Iron Mountain.
* Improved combo transitions.
* Enhanced dodge-to-counter opportunities.

Upgrades:

* More fluid animation transitions.
* Stronger, clearer impact feedback.
* Improved combo routing.
* A subtle aura that appears during successful counters.

Gameplay identity: A technical fighter who punishes predictable opponents.

Degree III — Grandmaster

The player’s movements become exceptionally precise and intimidating.

Unlock:

* Sweeping Dragon.
* Hundred Fists.
* Advanced combo finishers.
* A temporary focus state.

Focus State — Stillness Before the Storm

The player briefly adopts a calm stance. Successful attacks and well-timed defensive actions build focus. Activating the state enhances visual impact and improves selected combo transitions for a limited duration.

Do not make the player invincible or allow uninterrupted infinite combos.

Visual identity:

* Stronger motion trails.
* Brief air distortions during heavy strikes.
* More dramatic counter effects.
* A distinctive martial arts aura.

Gameplay identity: A versatile duelist who can adapt to different opponents.

Degree IV — Ascendant Dragon

The martial artist transcends ordinary physical limitations through supernatural mastery.

Unlock:

* Enhanced versions of existing techniques.
* An advanced counter follow-up.
* A powerful ultimate ability.

Passive — Perfect Form:

Consecutive successful attacks and well-timed defensive actions build a mastery meter. Taking damage, missing committed attacks, or losing momentum can reduce it.

The meter rewards precision, not mindless aggression.

Visual identity:

* Controlled golden-white or jade energy around the hands and feet.
* Subtle dragon-shaped energy trails during selected finishers.
* Distinctive stance and idle animation.
* Cinematic effects reserved for the strongest attacks.

Gameplay identity: A highly technical fighter with powerful rewards for consistent execution.

4. ULTIMATE ABILITY — HEAVEN-SPLITTING DRAGON

The player channels their accumulated martial mastery into a cinematic close-range finisher.

Sequence:

1. The player enters a focused stance as the surrounding sound briefly dampens.
2. A successful opening strike staggers the target.
3. The player performs a rapid sequence of punches, elbows, knees, and spinning kicks.
4. Each confirmed impact creates a directional shockwave and a brief flash of energy.
5. The player finishes with a powerful rising palm strike surrounded by a spectral dragon-shaped energy effect.
6. The target is launched backward as the arena briefly reacts with a controlled shockwave.

Requirements:

* Charge the ultimate meter through combat.
* Require a valid target within range.
* Include a clear startup animation.
* Allow the opening to be dodged or interrupted.
* Keep the cinematic short enough that the victim and nearby players are not forced to watch an excessively long sequence.
* Apply damage through server-authoritative hit checks.
* Prevent multiple players from repeatedly triggering the same target’s ultimate simultaneously.

The ultimate should feel spectacular because of its animation, timing, audio, and impact—not simply because it removes all of an opponent’s health.

5. OPTIONAL PREMIUM TECHNIQUE

If the game includes Robux purchases, consider a separate cosmetic or alternate-technique bundle called Celestial Dragon Style.

Potential contents:

* Alternate martial arts animations.
* Unique stance and victory poses.
* Special impact particles.
* A dragon-themed ultimate animation.
* Additional cosmetic aura options.

Keep the base Grandmaster power fully playable without paying. Premium purchases must not provide an overwhelming competitive advantage.

If selling a combat technique, disclose its gameplay function clearly and balance it against the standard abilities.

6. ANIMATION AND GAME FEEL

This power will succeed or fail based on how the combat feels.

Implement:

* Smooth transitions between idle, movement, attacks, dodges, blocks, counters, and knockdowns.
* Animation priorities that prevent conflicting tracks.
* Directional attacks that respond to player input.
* Small hit-stop effects on confirmed heavy impacts.
* Camera shake scaled to attack strength.
* Layered punch, kick, whoosh, and impact audio.
* Consistent hitboxes aligned with the character’s actual animation.
* Appropriate recovery frames and attack commitment.
* Readable effects that do not obscure opponents.
* Distinct hit reactions for light attacks, heavy attacks, launchers, and counters.

Avoid robotic animations, exaggerated ragdolling on every hit, oversized effects, and attacks that visually connect without registering damage.

Use custom animations where possible. If suitable assets are unavailable, create a clear placeholder system and document exactly which animations need replacement.

7. ROBLOX ENGINEERING REQUIREMENTS

Build the system using maintainable, modular Luau.

* Use the existing game’s architecture and power-degree framework wherever possible.
* Inspect the current codebase before creating or replacing systems.
* Do not overwrite unrelated powers or core combat systems.
* Separate client-side input and cosmetic effects from server-authoritative combat logic.
* Validate attack cooldowns, target distance, hit timing, damage, meter changes, and unlock requirements on the server.
* Prevent client exploits involving fabricated hits, impossible cooldowns, or unauthorized ability activation.
* Use appropriate Roblox services and reliable networking.
* Clean up temporary hitboxes, effects, connections, and animation state.
* Support character respawning and ability-state resets.
* Avoid excessive per-frame loops, unnecessary physics objects, and expensive particle spam.
* Provide configurable damage, cooldown, range, startup, recovery, knockback, and meter values.
* Ensure effects and animations remain performant on mobile devices.
* Respect existing stun, block, dodge, invulnerability, and ragdoll systems.

Never trust the client to decide whether an attack successfully hits another player.

8. BALANCE AND COMPETITIVE INTEGRITY

The Grandmaster should be dangerous without becoming unbeatable.

* Basic attacks should be quick but deal modest damage.
* Heavy attacks should have meaningful startup and recovery.
* Counters should reward timing rather than grant permanent protection.
* Crowd-control abilities should have appropriate diminishing returns or immunity windows.
* Ultimate abilities should require meaningful meter investment.
* Opponents should have opportunities to block, dodge, interrupt, or punish predictable attacks.
* Test against melee, ranged, defensive, and mobility-focused powers.
* Prevent infinite combo loops and repeated knockdown locking.
* Make progression feel rewarding without making earlier degrees useless.

Tune all numerical values through playtesting rather than assuming the first implementation will be balanced.

9. DEVELOPMENT WORKFLOW

Before implementation:

1. Inspect the existing project structure, combat framework, input system, animation assets, power-degree progression, and UI.
2. Identify reusable systems and compatibility risks.
3. Present a concise implementation plan and proposed file changes.
4. Implement the power in logical, testable modules.
5. Integrate the abilities with the existing progression and combat systems.
6. Test each ability independently, then test full combos and multiplayer interactions.
7. Fix errors, exploits, animation conflicts, and performance problems.
8. Provide a final report with changed files, ability controls, balance settings, known limitations, and testing results.

Do not claim an ability is finished or tested unless it has actually been implemented and verified in the Roblox environment.

10. FINAL QUALITY STANDARD

The finished result should feel like a premium martial arts combat system in a high-quality Roblox arena fighter.

The player should feel the difference between a beginner throwing punches and a Grandmaster controlling a fight through spacing, timing, defensive reads, combo execution, and mastery.

Prioritize responsive controls, satisfying combat, polished animations, meaningful progression, multiplayer fairness, and reliable code over unnecessary complexity.

Build a martial arts power that players want to master—not just another character with six random attacks.
